# FCU app 1.0.0 beta 2

**For testers.** From this release on, every test release is named by its **version** (here `1.0.0-beta.2`), comes with these notes, and the app shows the version on Settings. The old numbered test builds ("build 25" and earlier) stop here.

## Install

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu.apk` | Open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway**. |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.2-unsigned.ipa` | Apple does not allow installing an unsigned app directly. Install it on your own phone with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID; the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). A TestFlight build comes once the Apple Developer account is set up. |

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release.

**If you have a numbered test build (build 25 or earlier), install this one by hand once:** download `fcu.apk` from this release and open it. It installs over your current app. After that, the app tells you itself when a newer version is ready ("FCU 1.0.0-beta.3 is ready to install").

## What is new

Things you choose are now kept on the server, so they stay after you close the app and follow you to another phone:

- **"Hide this post"** hides it from your feed and from search for good. Undo brings it back.
- **"Pass" on a question** in *Questions for you* is remembered.
- **"Encouraged" on a testimony** shows the real number of people, one tap per person. It appears only once a story is published.
- **Your reflection on a devotional** is saved privately. Only you can read it. Clearing the text removes it.
- **Your last quiz result** and your own answers are kept, so *Review my answers* works after reopening the app.
- **Documents** in Resources open in your phone's viewer (browser or a PDF app), where you can also save them. Before, the page showed placeholder pages and a "coming soon" Download button.
- Quizzes with fewer than four answers per question now open normally.
- Settings shows the real app version (it always said "1.0" before).

## What to test

1. **Hide a post,** close the app, open it again: the post stays hidden. Tap Undo on another one: it comes back.
2. **Questions for you:** Pass on a question, close and reopen the app: it stays out of the list.
3. **Testimony:** tap *Encouraged* on a published story from two accounts: the count goes to 2. Your own story waiting for review has no Encouraged button.
4. **Devotional:** write a reflection, close the app, reopen the devotional: your text is there. Sign in as someone else: they do not see it.
5. **Quiz:** finish one, close the app, open the quiz result again and use *Review my answers*.
6. **Document:** open one from Resources and tap *Open document*.
7. **Settings:** the bottom of the page says *Version 1.0.0-beta.2*.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Close the app and you are signed out.** The session is not saved on the phone yet.
- **You cannot change your username** after signing up.
- **Search for people** only finds people in your own communities.
- **iPhone:** sideloading only (see Install) and no update banner; TestFlight needs an Apple Developer account.

## For the FCU team before sending this out

- **Update the server first.** This version needs the API from the same commit: run `npm run db:deploy` (adds migration `0006_post_marks_encouragements_reflections`) and restart the API. On the old server, hiding, passing, Encouraged, reflections and quiz results fail.
- The app talks to `https://apis.focusuganda.org/v1`. If testers use a different server, set the `API_BASE_URL` repository variable before building.
- Publish from **Actions > Mobile build > Run workflow > Publish**.
