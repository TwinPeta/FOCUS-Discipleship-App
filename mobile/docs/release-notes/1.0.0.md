# FCU app 1.0.0

**The first full version of the FCU app.** It works offline, it is much smaller and faster to install and open, and it has a new bottom bar.

## Install

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu.apk` | **Uninstall the beta first** (it was signed differently), then open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway**. You only uninstall this once: from 1.0.0 on, every update installs on top, and the app offers it itself (banner on Home, or **Settings > App updates**). |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa` | Install it with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID; the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). |

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release.

## What is new since 1.0.0 beta 6

- **Works offline.** Everything you have opened is saved on your phone and shows without internet: Home, your caregroup, prayer wall, stories, resources, pathways, meetings, messages, notifications and your profile. Photos you have seen show too.
- **Post, pray, comment and message offline.** Posts and questions (with a photo), comments and answers, likes, prayer requests, "I prayed", responses, stories (photo or video), messages, "I am going", reflections, finishing lessons and pathway steps, and profile changes are saved on your phone and **sent automatically** when you are back online. The offline banner says how many are waiting.
- **Opening the app offline keeps you signed in.** It no longer sends you back to the sign-up page.
- **Much smaller and faster.** The Android file is about **40 MB instead of 171 MB**, so it downloads and installs much faster, and the app opens faster (especially the first time).
- **New bottom bar.** One seamless white bar. The page you are on rises out of it as a white circle with a blue icon and its name. Other icons are outlines.
- **"Join"** is the button on a caregroup's page.
- **More stable.** If part of a screen ever fails, a small "Something went wrong here" note shows instead of a blank area, and the app keeps running.
- **Uses less phone storage and memory.** Temporary photo copies are cleaned up, saved photos are capped at 100 MB, and old saved content is cleared after 30 days.

## What to test

1. **Offline reading:** open Home, your caregroup feed and the prayer wall, then turn on airplane mode and close and reopen the app. You should stay signed in and see the saved content and photos.
2. **Offline writing:** still offline, write a post with a photo, comment, tap "I prayed" and send a message. They show at once. Turn airplane mode off: within a few seconds they are sent (pull to refresh to see them from the server).
3. **Sign out with something waiting:** go offline, post something, then Settings > Sign out. The app warns that unsent items will be deleted.
4. **Bottom bar:** switch between Home, Caregroups, Messages and Profile in the light and dark themes.
5. **Install and open speed:** note how long the download, install and first opening take.

## Known limits

- **Only what you opened is saved.** A page you never opened while online cannot show offline.
- **Meeting check-in and quizzes need internet** (the server checks the time and marks the answers).
- **If the server refuses something written offline** (for example, it was too long), it is not sent and the app does not tell you yet.
- **Points (XP)** are still shown during sign-up only.
- **iPhone:** sideloading only, so there's no App updates button.

## For the FCU team before sending this out

- **Server update (recommended):** deploy the latest API. Offline writes are then never stored twice, and live updates use the safer sign-in. Before deploying, make sure `JWT_SECRET` on the server is **at least 32 characters**, or the new API will refuse to start. The deploy scripts already create a 96-character one.
- **Nginx:** re-run the deploy script, so live-update connections are no longer written to the access log.
- **Signing key:** from this version, Android builds are signed with the permanent FCU key (GitHub secrets `FCU_KEYSTORE_*`). Keep its backup safe. Losing it means everyone has to uninstall again.
- Publish from **Actions > Mobile build > Run workflow > Publish**.
