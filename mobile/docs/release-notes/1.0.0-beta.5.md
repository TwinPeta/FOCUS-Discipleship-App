# FCU app 1.0.0 beta 5

**For testers.** You now stay signed in, and new messages and notifications appear on their own without pulling to refresh.

## Install

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu.apk` | If you have 1.0.0-beta.4, the app offers this update itself (banner on Home, or **Settings > App updates**). Otherwise open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway**. |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.5-unsigned.ipa` | Install it with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID; the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). TestFlight comes once the Apple Developer account is set up. |

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release.

## What is new

- **Sign in once.** Close the app, restart the phone, come back next week: you are still signed in. You are only asked to sign in again if you tap **Sign out** (or staff remove your access). Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage.
- **Live messages and notifications.** New messages, notifications and join-request updates arrive the moment they are sent. The app no longer keeps asking the server, which also saves battery and data.
- **Sign in your way.** The sign-in box takes your email, phone number or username, the same as the website. After too many wrong tries it tells you to wait a few minutes instead of showing a generic error.
- **Use your username instead of your name.** In **Settings**, turn on **Show my username**. Other students then see `@username` (and no photo) on your posts, comments and messages instead of your name. The FCU team can still see who you are, to keep everyone safe. It is off until you turn it on.
- **Sign-up checks your email early.** If an email is already registered you find out on the first step, not at the end.
- **Same account on the web.** The student website now has the new look and the same sign-in and sign-up as the app.

## What to test

1. **Stay signed in:** sign in, fully close the app (swipe it away), open it again. You should land on Home without a sign-in screen. Do the same after restarting the phone.
2. **Sign out:** use **Sign out** in the app, then reopen the app. It should ask you to sign in.
3. **Live updates:** sign in on two devices (or the app and the website) with two accounts. Send a message from one: it should appear on the other within a second or two without refreshing. Same for a notification.
4. **Offline:** turn on airplane mode, open the app. You should not be signed out (screens that need the internet may show an error until the connection is back).
5. **Sign in with username:** sign out, then sign in with `@username` and with your phone number.
6. **Username instead of name:** **Settings > Show my username** on, then post or message from that account. Another student should see `@username` and no photo; you still see your own name in Settings. Turn it off and the name returns.
7. **Update:** on 1.0.0-beta.4, **Settings > App updates** finds this version and installs it over your current app.

## Known limits in this beta

- **You cannot change your username** after signing up.
- **iPhone:** sideloading only, so no App updates button (it explains this instead).
- If the connection drops, the app checks for new items every so often until it reconnects, so a message can take a little longer to appear.

## For the FCU team before sending this out

- Deploy the latest API first (it must include the username setting, or the switch will not save), and set `REFRESH_TOKEN_TTL_DAYS=365` in the production `.env`. Without it students are asked to sign in again sooner than a year of inactivity. No database migration is needed.
- Publish from **Actions > Mobile build > Run workflow > Publish**.
