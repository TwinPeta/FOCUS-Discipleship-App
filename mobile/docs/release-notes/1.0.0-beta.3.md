# FCU app 1.0.0 beta 3

**For testers.** A new app icon, an **App updates** button on Settings, and clearer messages when creating an account fails.

## Install

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu.apk` | If you have 1.0.0-beta.2, the app offers this update itself (banner on Home, or **Settings > App updates**). Otherwise open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway**. |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.3-unsigned.ipa` | Install it with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID; the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). TestFlight comes once the Apple Developer account is set up. |

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release.

## What is new

- **New app icon:** the FCU flame, on Android (including round and themed icons on Android 13+), iPhone and the web.
- **Settings > App updates** (Android): tap it to check for a newer version at any time. If one is ready, it downloads straight away and Android offers to install it over your current app. Your account and settings stay. If you are up to date, it says so. If the phone is offline, it says the check failed, not that you are up to date.
- **Creating an account says what went wrong.** Before, the last step ("Join my campus") always said *"We could not create your account. Try again."*, whatever the cause, so trying again never helped. Now it says:
  - **This email already has an account,** with a **Sign in** button (use *Forgot password* if needed). This is the usual cause when an earlier try got further than it looked.
  - **The username is taken,** with **Edit details**. Your answers are kept.
  - **Which detail needs fixing** (for example the year of study).
  - **No connection** or **too many tries,** so you know to wait instead of changing your details.

## What to test

1. **Icon:** after installing, the home screen shows the yellow flame on a dark background. On Android 13+, turn on *Themed icons*: the flame follows your wallpaper colours.
2. **Settings > App updates:** shows *Version 1.0.0-beta.3 · Tap to check*. Tap it: *You have the latest version.* Turn on aeroplane mode and tap again: *Could not check for updates.*
3. **Sign up with an email that already has an account:** the last step says so and **Sign in** opens the sign-in page.
4. **Sign up with a username someone already has:** **Edit details** goes back to the form with your answers still there.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Close the app and you are signed out.** The session is not saved on the phone yet.
- **You cannot change your username** after signing up.
- **iPhone:** sideloading only, so no App updates button (it explains this instead).
