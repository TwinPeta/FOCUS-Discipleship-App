# FCU app 1.0.0 beta 1

**For testers.** This is the first build that talks to the real FCU server instead of made-up data. Anything you do (sign up, post, pray, message) is saved on the server and is seen by the other testers.

## Download

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.1.apk` | Open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway** (the beta is not on the Play Store yet). |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.1-unsigned.ipa` | Apple does not allow installing an unsigned app directly. Install it on your own phone with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID, the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). A TestFlight build, which installs like any App Store app, comes later. |

If you cannot sideload, tell the team: we can add you to TestFlight once the Apple Developer account is set up.

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release.

## What is new

The whole app now uses the real backend:

- **Sign up and sign in** with an email code. Under-18 sign-ups add a guardian afterwards and are limited to messaging other students and verified mentors until the guardian is confirmed.
- **Communities:** your General community, finding and requesting one specific community, leaving it, and the approved / not accepted screens. Super-mentors approve or decline requests and see who needs a follow-up.
- **Feed:** posts with photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, up to 2 MB), likes and comments.
- **Prayer wall:** requests (also anonymous), "I prayed", replies. Anonymous stays anonymous, even when the owner replies.
- **Testimonies:** share a story; it is reviewed before others see it.
- **Learn:** resources, quizzes (scored by the server), discipleship pathways, My activity and badges.
- **Meetings:** next meeting, RSVP, and check-in (opens 30 minutes before the start, closes 2 hours after).
- **Messages:** conversations, unread counts, blocking and reporting.
- **Notifications** and their settings: join request decisions, someone prayed for you, replies to your posts and prayer requests.
- **Safety:** super-mentors review reported content (keep, hide, warn the author, or pass it to the FCU team). Warnings you receive can be read and acknowledged. A suspended account can read but not post.

## What to test

Please try each of these with a real second person or a second phone, and write down anything that looks wrong:

1. **Join a community:** sign up, request a specific community, have a super-mentor approve it, and check that you see the result screen and a notification.
2. **Post with a photo,** like it from the other phone, comment on it.
3. **Prayer:** post one normally and one anonymously; reply from another account; check the owner of the anonymous one is never named.
4. **Meeting:** RSVP; try to check in too early (it should refuse) and at the meeting time (it should work, once).
5. **Quiz:** finish one; the score appears at the end (the app no longer shows the right answer after each question).
6. **Messages:** send a message, block the person, report them.
7. **Report a post** as a member; as the super-mentor warn the author and check the author gets a warning and a notification.
8. **Airplane mode:** open the feed, then turn the network off. You should see the last loaded copy and a banner.
9. **Settings:** change notification settings; delete your account last (this cannot be undone).

## Known limits in this beta

- **Close the app and you are signed out.** The session is not saved on the phone yet.
- **Forgot password** sends an email but there is not yet a way to set the new password.
- **New messages appear when you open a screen,** not live.
- **Notification emails are not sent,** only the in-app list. SMS is not sent at all.
- **Profile photo** cannot be uploaded yet (post, testimony and report photos can).
- **You cannot change your username** after signing up.
- The **"Encouraged"** tap on testimonies and your **devotional reflections** are kept only until you close the app.
- "Hide this post" is remembered only until you close the app.
- The school list in the sign-up form is built in. A school that has not been added by the FCU team is refused.
- The debug/test scenarios screen from earlier test builds is not in this build. To test super-mentor features you need an account the FCU team has made a super-mentor.
- The app icon and splash screen are still the Flutter defaults.

## For the FCU team before sending this out

- The server must be running the matching version: run `npm run db:deploy` (migrations `0002_notifications` and `0003_meeting_attendance`) and restart the API.
- Add the schools and communities testers will use in the back office, and make at least one super-mentor.
- The app talks to `https://apis.focusuganda.org/v1`. If testers use a different server, rebuild with the `API_BASE_URL` repository variable set.
