# FCU app 1.0.0 beta 6

**For testers.** The new **Carebook** look: new colours everywhere, communities are now **caregroups**, the sign-up gives you **points (XP)** and a **Newcomer** badge, and the **Bible characters / Bible texts** boxes are back on Resources.

## Install

| Phone | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `fcu.apk` | If you have 1.0.0-beta.5, the app offers this update itself (banner on Home, or **Settings > App updates**). Otherwise open the file on the phone and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app when asked. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised: choose **Install anyway**. |
| **iPhone** | `fcu-1.0.0-beta.6-unsigned.ipa` | Install it with **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** (free, uses your own Apple ID; the app stops opening after 7 days and must be installed again). |

Check the file you downloaded against the `SHA-256` list at the bottom of this release. Screenshots of the main screens in the light and dark themes are attached to this release.

## What is new

- **New colours (Carebook design):** navy text on a cream page, gold buttons, and blue for anything selected (tabs, chips, the active bottom-bar item, checkboxes, progress). Pages such as Settings, Help, Messages and Notifications have a blue-to-yellow header. The **+** (Create) button is a gold square in the middle of the bottom bar. The dark theme follows the same design. Light is the default.
- **Communities are now caregroups** in every screen ("General caregroup", "Find a specific caregroup", "Join caregroup", "Leave caregroup", "Caregroup charter"…).
- **Caregroup header:** a clean white bar with the name and where you are, and round **Search** and **⋯** buttons. Every section (and, on Resources, the type filter) is in the **⋯** menu. Nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Points in the sign-up:** each step shows your points so far and what the next step earns (10 + 10 + 10 + 20). At the end a new screen says **"You are all set"**, unlocks the **Newcomer** badge and shows **+50 XP · Level 1**, with **Join my campus** or **Complete my profile**.
- **Resources:** the **Bible characters** and **Bible texts** boxes are back. Tap one to show only that topic, and tap it again to show everything.
- **Joining a specific caregroup is instant:** the page says *"You join instantly and can leave at any time."*
- **Posts and prayer:** like and comment counts are in pale-blue pills. Questions have a gold **Answer** button, and prayers a gold **I prayed** button.
- **Landing page:** gold hero with a blue circle and a blue "Grow. Succeed.".

## What to test

1. **Light and dark:** Settings > Appearance. Go through Home, Caregroups, a caregroup's feed, the prayer wall, Messages, Profile and Settings in both. Everything should be readable.
2. **⋯ menus:** in a caregroup, open **⋯** and switch sections. On Resources, use **⋯** next to the filter to pick Videos, Devotionals, Quizzes or Documents.
3. **Bible boxes:** on Resources, tap **Bible characters**, then **Bible texts**, then the same box again.
4. **Sign up (new account):** check the XP pill and the "+XP when you continue" line on each step, and the **You are all set** screen at the end. Both buttons should work.
5. **Join a specific caregroup:** the button says **Join caregroup** and you are in straight away.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Points are shown during sign-up only.** They are not stored on your account yet, so they don't appear on your profile.
- **iPhone:** sideloading only, so there's no App updates button.

## For the FCU team before sending this out

- No server change is needed. Instant join uses the back office setting **Settings > Join requests > Auto-approve**, which is on by default. Keep it on. If it is switched off, joining goes back to "Request to join" and waits for the super-mentor.
- Publish from **Actions > Mobile build > Run workflow > Publish**.
